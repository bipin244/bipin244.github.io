Flagship case study

Paperly School Management Platform

Education SaaS · Perth, Australia · Oct 2021 – Aug 2026

My roleSenior Web Developer — lead architecture
Scope10+ production modules, end-to-end

Problem

Schools were running enrolment, excursions, co-curricular sign-ups, parent-teacher interviews and timetabling on manual, paper-based processes — slow to administer, easy to lose, and invisible to administrators.

What I contributed

  • Lead architecture and full-stack development, owning system design end-to-end.
  • A dynamic form builder with configurable multi-step approval workflows.
  • An availability-based auto-timetable generator for tutors and students.
  • Parent-teacher interview scheduling, enrolment, excursions and co-curricular forms.
  • A parent self-service details module gated by an admin-approval step.

Business value

Replaced manual, paper-based processes school-wide, removed manual re-entry across platforms, and increased operational visibility for school administrators.

Engineering challenges

  • Modelling configurable multi-step approval workflows generically enough to drive many different forms.
  • Generating timetables from tutor and student availability constraints.
  • Keeping verified parent data consistent with downstream systems over third-party APIs.
  • Sustaining performance and reliability through targeted architecture refactors as the platform grew.
